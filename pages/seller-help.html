Seller Help Guide

Simple instructions for using your seller dashboard.

How to log in

1
Go to the Sell with us page and click Log in to my dashboard.
2
Enter your WhatsApp number and click Send me a login code.
3
Ask the admin for the 6-digit code (they will send it to you on WhatsApp).
4
Enter the code and click Log in.

Submitting an item

1
Click the + Submit item button.
2
Fill in the item name, description, your minimum price, and upload a clear photo.
3
Click Submit for review.
4
Your item will appear under Submitted for review with status “Pending review”.

Understanding the statuses

  • Pending review — We are still checking your item.
  • ✓ Approved — Your item has been accepted and is now listed in an auction.
  • Rejected — We could not accept the item (you will usually be contacted with the reason).

Refreshing your dashboard

After you submit an item, or after the admin approves it, click the ↻ Refresh button at the top of your dashboard to see the latest information.

My items & Payments

My items shows everything that has been approved and listed in an auction.

Payments & payouts shows what you are owed and what has already been paid to you.

Need help?

Contact the C-Auctions team on WhatsApp if you have any questions about your items or payments.
