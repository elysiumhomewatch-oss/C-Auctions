Admin Help Guide

Simple step-by-step instructions for running the C-Auctions platform.

1. Managing Sellers

Adding a new seller

1
Go to the Sellers page.
2
Click + Add manually.
3
Enter the seller’s name and WhatsApp number, then save.

Approving a seller registration

When someone registers on the public “Sell with us” page, they appear with status Pending. Click the approve button to activate their account.

Sending a login code

1
On the Sellers page you will see a section called Login codes to send.
2
Copy the 6-digit code and send it to the seller on WhatsApp (or click the WhatsApp button).
3
The seller enters the code on their side to open their dashboard.
Codes expire after 10 minutes. If a seller says the code doesn’t work, ask them to request a new one.

2. Reviewing Item Submissions

Sellers submit items from their dashboard. These appear as Pending review.

1
Open the item request.
2
Decide whether to Approve or Reject.
3
If approving, choose which auction the item belongs to and set the starting bid.

Once approved, the item moves into the chosen auction and the seller will see it as “✓ Approved” after they refresh their dashboard.

3. Managing Auctions

1
Go to the Auctions page.
2
Create a new auction or open an existing one.
3
Add items (either from approved seller submissions or manually).
4
When ready, change the auction status from Draft → Live.
5
When the auction ends, close individual items (this marks them as Sold or Unsold and creates payment records).

4. Payments & Payouts

Buyer payments

When an item is sold, a payment record is created. Mark it as Paid once the buyer has paid you.

Seller payouts

1
Go to Payments & payouts.
2
You will see amounts owed to each seller (only for payments already marked as paid by the buyer).
3
Create a payout for the seller and later mark it as Sent once you have transferred the money.

5. Item Collection (QR / PIN system)

After a buyer has paid, you can generate collection PINs:

1
Open the payment and generate the collection tokens.
2
Give the buyer their PIN and the seller their PIN (or let them scan the QR codes).
3
When either party confirms the PIN on the collection page, the item is marked as collected.

You can also use the Scan QR page if you are handling collection in person.

6. Reports & Settings

Reports – View summaries of sales, payments and activity.

Settings / Config – Change platform-wide options (commission percentage, etc.).

Quick Tips

  • Always refresh the page (or click any Refresh button) after making important changes so you see the latest data.
  • Login codes for sellers expire after 10 minutes — just generate a new one if needed.
  • Only mark a buyer payment as “Paid” after you have actually received the money.
  • Only create a seller payout after the related buyer payments are marked Paid.

Need technical help?

Contact the person who set up the system if something is not working as expected.
